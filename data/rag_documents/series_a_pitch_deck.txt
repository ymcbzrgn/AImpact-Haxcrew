# How to Create a Winning Series A Pitch Deck
Source: Embroker & Industry Best Practices

## Overview
A pitch deck is a presentation founders use to showcase their startups to investors when raising money. For Series A funding specifically, it's critical because the business is still young and relatively unproven.

## The True Purpose of Your Pitch Deck
The primary goal isn't to convince investors to fund you. Instead, aim to spark genuine interest that leads to deeper conversations. Think of it like a first date rather than a sales pitch.

Your presentation should communicate three core elements: what you've accomplished, your future plans, and how funding enables those goals.

## Ten Core Sections (Guy Kawasaki Framework)
Limit your deck to maximum ten slides, entire pitch lasting no more than twenty minutes.

### 1. Value Proposition
Craft a single, clear sentence explaining what your business does. Example: "Insuring Companies Doing Incredible Things."

### 2. Market and Opportunity
Define your market size and potential customer base. Investors prefer specific, reachable markets over vague large opportunities.

### 3. Problem and Solution
Center customers in your narrative. Show real-life context through customer journeys demonstrating how your solution improves their lives.

### 4. Product or Service
Demonstrate functionality through videos, screenshots, or demos. Include customer testimonials.

### 5. Traction
Highlight momentum through customer response, revenue growth, user engagement, or meaningful milestones.

### 6. Team
Spotlight exceptional team members, particularly those with prior startup success.

### 7. Revenue Model
Explain current monetization and future plans. Address pricing strategy and customer acquisition costs.

### 8. Competition
Acknowledge competitors and articulate your distinct advantages within the competitive landscape.

### 9. Marketing and Sales
Outline strategies for sustaining and accelerating growth. Explain differentiation and customer conversion approaches.

### 10. Financials
Include income statements, sales forecasts, cash flow status, and funding requests. Research shows investors spend the most time viewing three slides: financials, team, and competition.

## Supporting Documentation
- Executive Summary: Detailed written version of your deck
- Detailed Financials: Three-year financial forecasts
- Technical Documents: For tech startups, materials experts can evaluate

## Delivering Your Best Pitch
- The Deck Must Stand Alone: Investors spend roughly 3 minutes 44 seconds reviewing decks independently
- Keep It Concise: Limit presentation to thirty minutes maximum
- Balance Storytelling with Data: Use customer examples to illustrate real-world impact
- Practice Extensively: Rehearse with colleagues and external audiences

## Key Insight
Most successful pitchers weren't naturally charismatic; they developed skills through deliberate practice.
